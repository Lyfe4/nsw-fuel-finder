Fuel Finder

Privacy

No account, no cookies, no analytics and no tracking.

Your location

“Use my location” is only used to find stations near you, and your position is never stored.

  • Your location is asked for only when you choose “Use my location”, and it is used in your browser.
  • Only a position rounded to about 1 km is sent to this site, in the search and its address. This site’s code does not store it or log it.

Searches, favourites and settings

  • A suburb or postcode you search for is sent to this site to find prices; its code does not store it.
  • Favourites and your light or dark choice are kept on this device. To show favourites’ prices, only their station numbers are sent.
  • The site is hosted by Netlify, which handles every request and counts requests from each internet address for a minute, to stop any one address overloading the site.

Other services

  • When the map is shown, your browser downloads it from OpenFreeMap, which sees your internet address and the area on screen. Nothing is fetched from OpenFreeMap until then.
  • “Directions” opens Apple Maps or Google Maps with the station as the destination. Your position is not passed on; the maps app finds it itself, if you let it.
  • Other sites linked from here are told only that you came from this site.
